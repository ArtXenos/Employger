Users

Login accounts and their roles. Administrators manage users and settings; HR managers manage payroll; general managers can view payroll; employees see only their own payslips. Every change is recorded in the audit log.

Password reset requests

Sent from "Forgot password?" on the login page. Check that it is really the person (in person or by phone) before you set a temporary password, then give it to them yourself. Dismiss a request you didn't expect.
